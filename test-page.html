A DIFFERENT APPROACH

Safety with the user in control

Online safety tools often make decisions for users. Our approach is different: identify potentially harmful language, explain what was detected, and let the user decide how they want to view it.

THE PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

Detect → Understand → Choose

Safety tools work best when people can see what happened and decide how to respond.

01

Detect

Local rules identify potentially harmful phrases without sending page text anywhere.

02

Understand

Clear categories and subtle markings show what was flagged while context stays readable.

03

Choose

Users can reveal the original content or view a local prototype safer alternative.

PRIVACY & PROTOTYPE SCOPE

Local by design for this sprint

Detection and safer alternatives currently use simple local rules. No page text is sent to an external AI service, API, backend, or database. Results are illustrative and may miss context or intent.
